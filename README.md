## The Solution & Business Impact

### The Problem
Traditional RBAC systems are rigid. If a "Manager" needs one extra feature, developers usually have to change code.

### Our Solution
-   **Atomic Permissions:** Granular control at the "action" level (e.g., `view_leads` is separate from `delete_leads`).
-   **Visual Admin Control:** Managers can toggle permissions for their teams without any technical knowledge.
-   **Hierarchical Safety:** A built-in fail-safe prevents users from granting more access than they personally hold.
